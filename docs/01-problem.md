# 01 — The problem

## The gap

Many people have a clear idea for a software product and no way to build it. A college department needs an equipment inventory system. A small shop needs a simple CRM. A student group wants a textbook exchange. Each of these is a standard business app, yet getting one built today means one of four poor options:

| Option | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| Hire developers | Too expensive and slow for small teams and individuals |
| No-code builders | Hit walls quickly on custom logic, roles and data rules; lock users into the vendor |
| AI chat assistants | Produce snippets, not a finished, connected, deployed product; the user still has to assemble it |
| AI app builders | Usually need paid AI credits, produce generic-looking prototypes, and keep the app on their own platform |

The result: the user is left with "a folder of code and good luck", a prototype that only looks finished, or a monthly bill they can't justify.

## The specific problems Nexora solves

1. **Turning an idea into a complete spec.** Non-technical users can't list every part a real product needs: roles, permissions, data relationships, validation, notifications, admin screens. The platform must infer these.
2. **Cost.** AI generation is expensive at volume. Many target users (students, colleges, small businesses in India and similar markets) can only use free AI tiers.
3. **Free-tier rate limits.** Free AI keys have small per-minute and per-day limits. A naive AI agent that loops until done hits those limits within the first hour and fails.
4. **Generic design.** AI-generated apps tend to look like the same template. Users want their logo, colors and a modern, deliberate look.
5. **Ownership.** Users want their code, data and hosting in their own accounts, not trapped in a builder.
6. **Trust.** Users can't judge code quality. They need a clear, honest answer to "is this ready to use?"
7. **Change after launch.** Requirements evolve. Users need to say "add employee roles" and have the existing app updated, not regenerated from scratch.

## Who it's for

| User | Typical app | What matters most |
| --- | --- | --- |
| College department or institute staff | Inventory, lab booking, attendance, requests | Roles and approvals, low cost, works on campus infrastructure |
| Small business owner | CRM, orders, stock, appointments | Their branding, simple admin dashboard, email notifications |
| Student or student group | Marketplaces, clubs, project tools | Free, fast, looks modern |
| Early-stage founder | SaaS MVP, internal tools | Real code they own and can hand to developers later |

Primary market for v1: India (users may need Telugu, Hindi, Tamil and other Indian scripts; payments later through Razorpay).

## What success looks like

A non-technical user can go from a one-paragraph description to a live, branded, tested business app in their own accounts, using only free AI keys, in the same day, and can keep changing it by asking in plain words.
